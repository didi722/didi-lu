How are these numbers calculated?
  • Global adds up every season except the Beta one, like the rest of the site.
  • Elo in the global view is the trainer's ranking; in a season it is the rating at the end of that season. Players are ordered by Elo (global) or by season points.
  • Seasons won: the trainer with the most points when a season closes. A team or a Pokémon gets the credit if it played at least one match in that season.
  • Showdowns: won by whoever wins more matches. A team (or a Pokémon of that team) gets the result of every showdown it played in.
  • Usage counts the species, not the single Pokémon: the share of the teams registered to the seasons that include it (the same team registered in two seasons counts twice). Moves, items, abilities and natures are shares of the sets of that Pokémon. Teams that were never registered to a season do not count.
  • Pokémon are the individual ones of each team (not the species): the Garchomp of one team and the Garchomp of another are different entries.
  • KOs come from the sets played on the site. Matches entered by hand have no KO data, so they count for results but not for KOs. A KO is credited to the Pokémon that caused it, also when it comes from poison, burn, entry hazards, weather, Leech Seed, Perish Song…; self-inflicted KOs (recoil, Life Orb) are credited to nobody.
  • KO share is the part of the team's credited KOs scored by that Pokémon. Last standing: it was the only Pokémon left on its team.
